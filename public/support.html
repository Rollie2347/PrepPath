Common questions

How do I switch between the SAT and the ACT?
On the Learn tab, tap the SAT or ACT badge at the top left. You can add the other test there too. Your progress for each test is kept.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?
Go to Profile and tap Sign in with Apple, then sign in with the same Apple ID on your new phone.

How do I report or block a player's name?
In the League tab, tap their name and choose Report or Block.

I think a question is wrong.
Email us the question text (a screenshot is fine) and we will check it.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your data. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Does PrepPath cost anything?
No. There are no ads, subscriptions or lesson limits.
