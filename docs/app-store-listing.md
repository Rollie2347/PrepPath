## Description (4,000)

PrepPath turns SAT and ACT prep into a few focused minutes a day. No ads, no subscriptions, no paywall, and no limits on how much you practice.

Start with a short diagnostic that finds your level, then follow a learning path built from the skills the real tests measure. Each lesson adapts as you go: questions get harder as you improve, and the ones you miss are saved so you can fix them later. A few lessons a day add up to real progress before test day.

BUILT FOR BOTH TESTS
• SAT: Math and Reading & Writing
• ACT: English, Math, Reading, and Science
• Studying for both? Add the second test and switch between them anytime from the home screen.

QUESTIONS THAT TEACH
• More than 1,200 original practice questions, written in the style of the current tests
• A step-by-step explanation for every question, so you learn why an answer is right, not just which one it is
• Clean math typesetting for fractions, exponents, radicals, and equations
• Reading passages, grammar edits, and science experiments with data tables to interpret

HOW IT WORKS
Your path is split into units, one skill at a time. Finish a lesson to unlock the next one. Checkpoints along the way make sure the skills stick before you move on. Already know a skill? Come back to any lesson to practice again, or take on its timed Legendary challenge to prove you've mastered it.

A GRAPHING CALCULATOR, BUILT IN
Graph functions, find where curves cross, locate zeros, and explore equations with sliders without leaving the app. It's there when a math question needs it, and you can open it from the Practice tab anytime.

PRACTICE YOUR WAY
• Weakest skills: drill your three lowest-rated skills
• Fix mistakes: retry every question you've missed
• Bookmarks: save tricky questions and come back to them
• Hardest questions: top difficulty only, for when you're ready for a challenge

SEE YOURSELF IMPROVE
Your estimated score updates as you practice and gets more precise the more questions you answer, so you can watch it climb on the way to test day.

STAY MOTIVATED
• Daily streaks, with streak freezes for the days life gets in the way
• Earn XP and set a daily goal that fits your schedule
• Daily quests and reward chests
• Gems to spend in the shop
• Weekly leagues: climb the leaderboard against players at your level and get promoted to the next tier

FREE MEANS FREE
Many prep apps limit how many questions you can answer, lock content behind a subscription, or interrupt you with ads. PrepPath does none of that. Every question, every practice mode, and the calculator are free for everyone.

YOUR PROGRESS, YOUR CHOICE
Start practicing right away without an account, or sign in with Apple to keep your progress safe across devices. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from the Profile tab.

WHO IT'S FOR
• High school students preparing for the SAT or ACT
• Students deciding which test to take who want to try both
• Students retaking a test to raise their score
• Anyone who wants steady daily practice instead of last-minute cramming

All questions are original and written for PrepPath. They are not official test questions.

## App Review information

**Sign-in required:** No. On the welcome screen, tap **Not now** to continue without an account. Sign in with Apple is optional.

**Notes for the reviewer:**

> PrepPath is a free SAT/ACT practice app with no ads or purchases.
> - No account is needed: tap "Not now" on the welcome screen, then finish the short setup.
> - Account deletion: Profile tab → "Delete account" (bottom of the screen).
> - Display names are visible to other players in weekly leagues. Names are filtered for offensive words. To report or block a player, open the League tab (it appears after the first completed lesson) and tap their name.
> - Privacy policy, terms, and support links are at the bottom of the Profile tab.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

**Data used to track you:** None.

**Data linked to you** (all for *App Functionality* only, none for advertising or tracking):

| Data type | Why |
|---|---|
| Contact Info → Name | The display name a player chooses, and the first name from Sign in with Apple, which is used only to suggest a display name |
| Identifiers → User ID | Firebase account ID |
| Usage Data → Product Interaction | Answers, lessons completed, XP, streaks, and league results |
| Other Data | Test goals, target scores, and test dates the player enters |

**Data not collected:** location, contacts, photos, browsing history, health, financial info, email address, and device advertising ID.

Third parties: Google Firebase processes this data on PrepPath's behalf, and jsDelivr serves the math-typesetting library (it receives only the IP address).
